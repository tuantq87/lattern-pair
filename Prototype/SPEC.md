# Lantern Pair — "Leo vách cùng đốm sáng"

Portrait 390×844 logical canvas, DPR-scaled, pointer events, works offline (`index.html` + `assets/`, no CDN). It runs on both http:// and file://.

- **style_id:** `flat`
- **audio:** `viral_pop_lowcortisol` + `asmr_satisfying_sfx`

## Core loop
A girl in a red cloak climbs between two cliff walls while darkness rises from below. Her round wisp companion acts as a one-hit shield and as a tool.

- **TAP** anywhere: wall-jump to the opposite wall on a fixed arc (rises 70 px, about 0.46 s). Each landing adds +1 to the chain, and every 10 in a row gives a bonus.
- **SWIPE UP** while airborne: dash straight up 150 px, which breaks roots. Cooldown is 2 s, shown as a ring on the HUD. A swipe started while clinging does jump + dash.
- **HOLD** (0.32 s, shown by a progress ring) on a glowing, dashed-outline object to send the wisp to it. Each use costs 20 energy.
  - door: shortcut, +210 px and brief invulnerability
  - mushroom: the next jump is a high bounce (+230 px)
  - rope bridge: rest; the darkness stops for 3 s and drops back 140 px
  - flower: blooms, gives +50 energy, and restores the shield
- **TAP a shadow crawler:** the wisp repels it (25 energy).

**Hazards**
- Rock spikes: landing on them is a hit.
- Breaking roots across the gap: a hit unless you dash through them, which gives +5.
- Sideways wind zones: a tailwind lengthens the arc, a headwind shortens it.
- Patrolling shadow crawlers.
- Darkness: it speeds up over time and with each tier. It rubber-bands so it never falls more than 600 px behind.

**Game over:** two hits (the first only breaks the wisp shield) or being caught by the darkness.

**Score** = height (m) + star seeds × 5 + chain/root bonus. The best score is stored per planet, plus an overall best.

## Planets & meta
- Each run is a procedural planet generated by mulberry32 from its seed. It gets a compound name (e.g. "Oslo-9"), a seed shown as "#4127", a 4-colour palette family, density, wind and crawler rates, darkness speed, music tonic and lead instrument, and planet type (banded, cratered or ringed, plus an optional moon).
- Every 150 m is a new ecological layer, with a name banner, palette hue shift and crossfade, a major chord, and a piano motif.
- There are 9 catalog planets. Planets 1–3 are open. The rest unlock with banked star seeds: 40, 80, 130, 190, 260 and 340. There is no real money.
- **NHẬP SEED** opens an in-canvas keypad. It replays any seed you have already visited and rejects unknown seeds. Recent seeds appear as chips.

## Audio direction (WebAudio, all synthesized, original)
Audio starts on the first touch. Mute is the round button at the top right and is saved in localStorage (`lantern-pair:muted`).

**BGM:** lo-fi 85 BPM, I–vi–IV–V.
- warm detuned pad, soft kick, brush and hat
- band-passed noise rain bed
- sine bass
- pentatonic lead

Each planet has its own tonic and lead instrument (Rhodes, pluck, bell or kalimba). A vibrating sawtooth bass layer with tremolo (41 Hz) fades in as the darkness gets close.

**SFX:**

| Event | Sound |
|---|---|
| bounce | pentatonic pluck that climbs with the chain |
| dash | noise whoosh |
| activation | chime + chord |
| seed | sparkle tick |
| crawler | low rumble |
| hit | low-passed thud |
| every 150 m | major chord + one of 4 original 4–6 note nocturne-style piano motifs (music ducks) |
| game over | falling notes |
| buttons | click / deny / unlock |

Style is inspired by lo-fi and ASMR conventions only; no music or SFX is copied.

## Hooks
- `window.G`: full state, including `mode`/`scene` and `score`.
- `window.__game`:
  - `ready()`, `info()`: mode, score, best, alt, stars, chain, shield, energy, gap, tier, planet, girl, on-screen objects and crawlers, button centres, bank, unl, visited, muted, audio
  - `plan()`: next real action, `{kind: tap|swipe|hold, x, y, x2, y2, why}`
  - `sim(s)`, `setTS(v)`, `press(id)`, `start(seed)`, `menu()`
  - `force.*`: test helpers
  - `bot(seed, policy)`, `balance(n, policy)`: restores all `lantern-pair:` localStorage afterwards
